What you get

Never lose a copy again

⌘C

Copy like always

Use ⌘C and ⌘X as you do now. Stash quietly saves text, links, colors, images and files.

⌃H

Pops up from the corner

Press ⌃H in any app and a Liquid Glass panel slides up from the bottom right. Change the shortcut any time.

⌕

Search & filter

Start typing to find anything. Filter by text, links, images, files or pinned.

↩

Paste for you

Pick a clip and it pastes right where you were typing. ⌘1–⌘9 paste the first nine instantly.

📌

Pin & auto-tidy

Keep the last 50 to 500 copies (100 by default). Older ones are deleted automatically; pinned clips stay forever.

🔐

Private by design

Your history never leaves your Mac, and copies from password managers are skipped.

How to use it

Copy now, paste whenever

Open Stash

A short tour shows you around (Next, Back or Skip). Stash then lives in your menu bar as a clipboard icon.

Copy as usual

Everything you copy with ⌘C or ⌘X is saved automatically.

Press ⌃H

Your history pops up. Type to search, choose with ↑ ↓, press ↩ to paste. Or just click a clip.

Allow pasting (optional)

Allow Accessibility once and Stash pastes for you. Without it, Stash copies the clip and you press ⌘V.

Keyboard
KeysWhat it does
⌃HOpen or close your history.
↑ ↓ then ↩Choose a clip and paste it.
⌘1 … ⌘9Paste one of the first nine clips.
⌘↩Copy only, without pasting.
⌘PPin or unpin a clip.
⌘⌫Delete a clip.
TabSwitch filter.
EscClear the search, or close.

Light on your battery: 0% CPU when idle and about 12 MB of memory. Stash only reads the clipboard when something new is copied.
