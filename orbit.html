Orbit

A calm focus timer with your music built in. Focus, take a break, repeat, with a glowing dial that keeps you on track.

What you get

Focus that feels good

◔

The dial

A big, glowing ring counts down. The last ten seconds glow brighter.

↻

Focus & breaks

Focus 25, short break 5, long break 15. Change any of them.

♫

Music built in

Control Spotify, Apple Music or YouTube, or play music files from your Mac.

⧈

Mini timer

A small floating timer keeps the countdown in view while you work.

3·2·1

Countdown start

A short 3·2·1 before each session. Turn it off if you like.

🎉

Celebrate progress

Finished sessions are celebrated and counted for today.

How to use it

One session at a time

Pick a mode

Focus (purple), Short Break (teal) or Long Break (blue).

Name your task

Type what you're studying so you stay on one thing.

Press start

3·2·1, then the dial counts down. Allow notifications to hear when it ends.

Add music

Whatever plays in Spotify, Music or YouTube shows up. Or drop audio files onto the music card.

Menu commands

First time only: macOS says it can't verify Orbit because it's not from the App Store. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. Full install guide →

🎵

The first time Orbit reads your music, macOS asks "Orbit wants to control Spotify" (or Music, Chrome, Safari). Click OK.
