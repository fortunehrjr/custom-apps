Install

Install in a minute

The same three steps work for every app. Here's exactly what you'll see, including the one-time "Open Anyway" step.

Open the installer

Download the app's .dmg from its page here, then double-click it. A window opens with the app and an Applications shortcut.

Drag it to Applications

Drop the app icon onto the Applications folder. Then eject the installer (the ⏏ next to it in Finder).

Open it

Open Applications and double-click the app. Menu bar apps (Clipper, Ferry, Now Playing) show an icon at the top right.

Allow it the first time

macOS says it "cannot verify" the app, because it's not from the App Store. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway, then confirm with your password or Touch ID. Only once.

Download an installer

Clipper.dmg Ferry.dmg Now Playing.dmg Orbit.dmg

All four are universal apps: they run on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

📤 Share with a friend

Send them the link to this website, or the .dmg itself by AirDrop or Messages. Every installer includes a "How to Install" note that walks them through the Open Anyway step.

🗑 Uninstall

Quit the app from its menu, then drag it from Applications to the Trash. To stop an app opening at login, also check System Settings › General › Login Items.
