What you get

Tidy windows without thinking about it

◧

Snap to the edges

Left or right edge for half the screen, corners for quarters, the top to fill it.

⌨︎

Keyboard shortcuts

⌃⌥ plus an arrow. Press again to cycle through ½, ⅔ and ⅓.

⇔

Resize together

Drag the edge between two windows and both follow, like a shared divider.

🔒

Lock in place

⌃⌥L pins a window. If anything moves it, it slides right back.

▦

Thirds and more

First, center and last thirds, two-thirds, center, fill and restore.

How to use it

Three moves to learn

Allow Accessibility

Clipper asks once. Switch it on in Privacy & Security › Accessibility. The welcome window carries on by itself.

Drag to an edge

A soft outline shows where the window will land. Let go and it snaps.

Use the peg

Everything (layouts, settings, the tour) is under the peg icon in your menu bar.

🔐
