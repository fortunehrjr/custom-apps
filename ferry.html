Everything Finder's copy bar doesn't tell you

How to use it

Copy like you always do

Install and open it once

Ferry starts by itself at login after that. Look for ⇅ in the menu bar.

Copy files in Finder

Drag footage to your SSD, or from a card to your Mac. Ferry pops up right away.

Watch it, or don't

Shrink it with the chevron, hide it with ✕. Ferry remembers your choice.

Done

It shows "Copy complete" with the average speed, then slips away. Past copies are in History.

First time only: macOS says it can't verify Ferry because it's not from the App Store. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. Full install guide →

🪟

Hiding Finder's window ("Ferry" mode) needs Accessibility permission: System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility, switch on Ferry.

⏸︎

Copies can be cancelled but not paused, because Finder doesn't let other apps pause its copies.
