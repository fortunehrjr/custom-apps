Getting started

Up and running in a minute

⬇︎

1. Open the installer

Download the app's .dmg from its page and double-click it.

↗︎

2. Drag to Applications

Drop the app onto the Applications shortcut in the window that opens.

✓

3. Allow it once

The first time, click Open Anyway in Privacy & Security. That's it.
